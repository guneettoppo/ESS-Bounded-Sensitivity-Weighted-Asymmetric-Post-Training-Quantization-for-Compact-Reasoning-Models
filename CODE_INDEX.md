### Verified invariants (so you know what "tested" means here)

1. `½tr(ΔHΔᵀ) − tr(Δ(WC_w)ᵀ)` equals `½‖(ΔX − W(X̃−X))D^{½}‖²_F` up to a constant — verified numerically to 5 significant figures.
2. `WeightedHessian` reproduces `X D Xᵀ` and `(X̃−X)D Xᵀ` computed densely — exact.
3. With a 16-bit grid the solver lands within 0.14 % of the unconstrained optimum `W + P` (vs 1.95 % from `W`), which is what proves the pre-shift sign and placement are right.
4. Every variant lowers the layer objective versus RTN (−10.1 % to −14.4 %) on a synthetic problem with heavy-tailed channels.
5. `w=None` and `w=ones` produce bit-identical quantised weights — the engineering gate.
6. JSD is symmetric (0.0 deviation), zero for identical distributions (2.6e-10), bounded by ln 2, and the top-k approximation converges to 0.01 % error at k=512.
7. ESS bisection hits 0.90 / 0.50 / 0.25 exactly and is monotone in β.
8. 3-bit and 4-bit code packing round-trips exactly; dequantisation matches `W_hat`.
